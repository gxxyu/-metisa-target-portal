CANDIDATE INTERVENTIONS

Candidate Targeting Strategies

Literature-supported inhibitor strategies mapped to the protein families represented in the final Top 10. These mappings are hypothesis-generating and do not demonstrate pesticide efficacy in Metisa plana. Experimental validation is required.

01

Trypsin / Peptidase S1

Final Top 10 proteins g13783.t1, g14298.t1, g13779.t1, g13784.t1, g14301.t1 and g18520.t1

Candidate targeting strategy Bowman-Birk proteinase inhibitors

Proposed targeting rationale Inhibition of trypsin/chymotrypsin-like serine proteases may interfere with larval dietary protein digestion.

Evidence status Literature-supported inhibitor mapping; validation in Metisa plana is required.
02

Triacylglycerol Lipase

Final Top 10 proteins g4082.t1, g4084.t1, g4083.t1 and g4086.t1

Candidate targeting strategy Tetrahydrolipstatin (THL; orlistat)

Proposed targeting rationale Lipase inhibition may reduce dietary lipid hydrolysis during larval feeding.

Evidence status Literature-supported inhibitor mapping; validation in Metisa plana is required.

Candidate targeting strategies are presented as computationally and literature-supported hypotheses. They should not be interpreted as confirmed pesticides or experimentally validated treatments for Metisa plana.
